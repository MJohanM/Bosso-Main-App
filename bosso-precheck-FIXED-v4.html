Terms & consent

Genuine documents only. By continuing, you confirm that you are the person named in these documents, and that every document you attach is genuine, unaltered, and belongs to you. Every document submitted here is checked, including automated authenticity and consistency checks. Submitting a false, altered, or fraudulent document is a criminal offence in South Africa and will result in your application being declined without further explanation, and may be reported to the relevant authorities and shared with other credit providers.

What this pre‑check is. This step gives you an initial decision on whether your documents can proceed to a full application with Bosso Loans (NCR reg. NCRCP19088). It is not itself a loan offer or final approval — final approval still depends on your full application, affordability assessment, and our standard lending criteria.

Your information. Your documents and personal information are used only to assess this application and are shared only with Bosso Loans staff for that purpose. Once our review is complete, this information is permanently deleted from our systems. If you proceed to a full loan application through our online portal, any documents you provide there are handled separately, as part of that platform.

International data transfer. As part of processing your application, your personal information (including your ID number, contact details, and the documents you upload) may be transferred to and stored on servers located outside South Africa, including within the European Union. We take reasonable steps to ensure this information is protected to a standard consistent with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA). By continuing, you consent to this transfer.

Credit check and legal status. By continuing, you consent to Bosso Loans performing a credit check on you as part of this application. You also confirm that you are not currently under debt review, that you have not applied for and are not currently under sequestration, and that you have not been declared insolvent.
